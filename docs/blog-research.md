# Blog research pipeline (SEO + GEO/AEO)

## How we know what people search
1. **Query mining (monthly, ~30 min):** run searches like
   "what pages does a mortgage website need", "website not converting",
   "how much does a website cost" and mine the "People also ask"/related
   sections; check autocomplete suggestions; note phrasing borrowers and
   small-business owners actually use.
2. **AI-engine angle (AEO):** ask ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity the target
   questions monthly; note what gets cited (structure, specificity, fresh
   dates) and what our pages lack. Answer-first posts with FAQ schema are
   the citable shape.
3. **Referrer watch (free, already built):** the dashboard's Traffic card
   logs referrers — chatgpt.com, perplexity.ai and gemini referrers will
   appear here once AI engines start citing us. That is our AEO feedback
   loop.
4. **Cadence:** 2 posts/month minimum, each answering ONE real query,
   opening with the direct answer in 2–3 sentences, FAQ block with
   matching schema, internal links to work/services pages.
